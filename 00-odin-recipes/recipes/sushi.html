Sushi Rolls

Description

Sushi rolls are rice, fish, and vegetables wrapped in seaweed and sliced into bite-sized pieces.

Ingredients

  • Sushi Rice
  • Dried seaweed
  • Seafood
  • Vegetables

How to Make a Simple Sushi Roll

  1. Prepare Rice: Cook sushi rice and season with rice vinegar.
  2. Lay Nori: Place nori on a bamboo mat, shiny side down.
  3. Add Rice: Spread a thin layer of rice, leaving a small border.
  4. Add Fillings: Place fish, vegetables, or desired fillings in a line.
  5. Roll: Use the mat to tightly roll the sushi, pressing firmly.
  6. Slice & Serve: Cut into pieces with a sharp knife and enjoy!
