Tacos

Description

Tacos are tortillas filled with meat, cheese, and toppings, offering endless customization.

Ingredients

  • Hard shell tacos
  • Ground beef
  • Salsa
  • Cheese
  • Vegetables

Cooking Instructions

  1. Cook the protein: In a skillet, cook the ground beef (or chicken) over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  2. Season: Add the taco seasoning (and water, if required by the packet) to the meat. Stir and cook for another 5 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas: Heat the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave for 15-20 seconds to soften them.
  4. Assemble tacos: Spoon the seasoned meat onto each tortilla. Top with shredded lettuce, cheese, and salsa.
  5. Optional toppings: Add sour cream and a squeeze of lime, if desired.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
