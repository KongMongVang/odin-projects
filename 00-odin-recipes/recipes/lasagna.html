Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a layered Italian dish with pasta, rich sauce, creamy cheese, and baked to golden, savory perfection.

Ingredients

  • Pasta Sheets
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Cheese
  • Ground beef

Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat Oven: 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook Meat Sauce: Sauté ground meat with onions and garlic, add marinara sauce, and simmer.
  3. Prepare Cheese Mixture: Mix ricotta, egg, parsley, and shredded cheese.
  4. Boil Noodles: Cook lasagna noodles until al dente, then drain.
  5. Layer Ingredients: In a baking dish, layer sauce, noodles, cheese mixture, and repeat. Top with mozzarella.
  6. Bake: Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake until cheese is golden (15-20 minutes).
  7. Rest & Serve: Let it sit for 10 minutes before slicing. Enjoy!
